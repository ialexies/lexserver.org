Indexwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Indexwise is a browser extension that analyzes the SEO of the web page you are viewing. This policy explains what it does with data.

Short version

Indexwise runs in your browser. It has no accounts, uses no analytics or ads, and does not send the pages you analyze to us or to anyone else.

What Indexwise reads

When you open Indexwise on a page, it reads that page's content in your browser: title, meta tags, headings, links, images, structured data, and similar SEO signals. This happens locally, and the results are shown only to you.

Requests Indexwise makes

To check a page properly, Indexwise makes requests from your browser to the website you are analyzing: the page itself, its /robots.txt, /llms.txt and sitemap, and, if the page lists alternate language versions, those pages. If you use the Bulk audit, it requests the URLs you list. If you watch a page, it re-checks that page's address every few hours. These requests go to the websites themselves, not to us.

What Indexwise stores

If you choose to watch pages, Indexwise stores the list of those pages and their last-known SEO tags in your browser's local extension storage. You can stop watching a page at any time, and uninstalling the extension deletes this data. Indexwise does not sync it to any server.

What Indexwise does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell personal data or browsing history.
  • It does not track you or use third-party analytics, ads or trackers.
  • It does not use remote code.

Permissions

Indexwise asks for permissions only to do the above: reading the active page when you click the icon, showing the side panel, saving your watched pages locally, running periodic checks, and notifying you of changes. Access to websites is requested only when you click to allow it.

Changes

If a future version collects or transmits data (for example a paid license check or an optional Google Search Console connection that you turn on), this policy will be updated before it ships and the change will be described here.
